Constellation with a pulse. B's typographic tree and mode shapes are the body, C's honest activity lane is the pulse, and A's pinned needs-you band and numbered findings are the voice. Every agent gets two rows: glyph, name, state word and elapsed · tokens, then a 12-cell lane of its last 60 seconds (▂ think ▅ tools █ write ▒ waiting on you) and one human sentence. When it finishes, the lane gives way to its finding and the file:line behind it. One amber band under the header carries the literal request and ^N. The run the chat shows gets the accent ▌ and the words 'in chat'. No ETA, no per-agent percentage and no operations: those live in the overlay.

^F (or ^Space where the terminal delivers it) shows the hints. Badges replace the tree connector and the state glyph stays. Agents that need you get the first letters, then s d f g h j k l, then w e r t u i o p. y a d n q never become badges. Digits 1-9 pick a run. ^F again = ^N, the next thing waiting on you. Esc cancels. Hints never answer anything. Overlay: Esc goes back to the exact chat scroll and draft, [ ] step through agents in panel order, Tab moves focus between band, activity and composer, Enter expands a group or sends a steer to this agent only, and o shows raw operations. Approvals use the existing grammar where the request is shown: y/a once, Y this run, A always, d deny, D deny + stop, n next. They work only while the composer is empty. ^B cycles full → compact → hidden (strip → off under 120 columns). /panel sets the mode and the choice persists.
